Loni's Sourdough Bread Recipe

Loni's passion for baking really comes through in this awesome sourdough recipe! After tons of experimenting, she landed on this one, and it's a total winner. The flavor and texture are seriously amazing – we're talking pure sourdough heaven. It's become a weekly thing in our kitchen, and we think you'll be baking it non-stop too. Give it a try and share the sourdough love!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup active sourdough starter
  • 2 cups unbleached all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup lukewarm water
  • teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Mix: In a large bowl, combine the starter, flour, and water. Mix until just combined. It will be a shaggy dough.
  2. Rest: Cover the bowl and let the dough rest for 30 minutes. This is called the "autolyse" and helps develop the gluten.
  3. Add Salt: Add the salt to the dough and mix it in thoroughly.
  4. First Rise (Bulk Fermentation): Cover the bowl again and let the dough rise at room temperature for 4-6 hours. Every hour, gently stretch and fold the dough by grabbing a portion, stretching it upwards, and folding it over the rest. This strengthens the dough.
  5. Shape: Gently turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface. Shape it into a round or oval loaf.
  6. Second Rise (Proofing): Place the shaped dough in a bowl or basket lined with a floured cloth. Cover and refrigerate overnight (at least 8 hours).
  7. Bake: Preheat your oven to 450°F (232°C) with a Dutch oven or heavy pot inside. Once hot, carefully place the dough in the Dutch oven (you can use parchment paper to prevent sticking). Cover and bake for 20 minutes. Then, remove the lid and bake for another 25-30 minutes, until golden brown and the internal temperature reaches 200°F (93°C).
  8. Cool: Let the bread cool completely on a wire rack before slicing and enjoying.

Note: Starter

A sourdough starter is a live culture of wild yeast and bacteria.If you don't have one, you can find instructions online for how to make one. It takes about a week. A good starter will be bubbly and double in size after feeding.
